About

I build end-to-end quantitative research pipelines: from raw market and macro data through feature engineering, rolling out-of-sample model evaluation, and portfolio-level analysis. My work emphasizes reproducible backtests, explicit risk overlays, and communication of methodology — not black-box performance claims.

Research principles

  • Out-of-sample evaluation first — rolling holdout and walk-forward protocols, not in-sample fit.
  • Transparent data lineage — versioned parquet pipelines, manifest reports, reproducible run scripts.
  • Risk-aware framing — stop-loss overlays, transaction-cost sensitivity, and clear limitation statements.

US sector allocation by rate, inflation & business cycle

Which US equity industries outperform under different macro regimes? Using Ken French 12 industry portfolios (Jun 1932 – Sep 2023), Shiller long interest rates, headline CPI, and FRED industrial production, this study maps conditional sector returns across rate direction, inflation level, and business-cycle phase — with historical episode review (1970s stagflation, GFC, QE era, 2021–23 hiking cycle) and fundamental rationale.

  • 1,096-month sample with expanding-history regime thresholds (no look-ahead on level cuts)
  • Energy leads in high-CPI and rising-rate regimes (+4.7 to +5.1pp excess vs equal-weight)
  • NonDurables / Health Care best Sharpe when INDPRO contracts; Energy lags (−7.3pp)
